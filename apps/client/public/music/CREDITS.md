# Instrument packs

The music's instrument packs (D-AUD-16) are single notes of the **Fluid R3 GM soundfont** by Frank Wen,
as rendered by [midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts) and released there under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). They are cut, levelled
and re-encoded by `tools/music-samples/build.ts`; the tunes they play are the game's own.

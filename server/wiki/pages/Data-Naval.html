Naval data

Every value in the game's naval config, exactly as the game reads it. This page is made from packages/rules/data/domain/naval.json each time the config changes, so it always matches the game. How these values are used is explained on Naval.

Keys are written as in the config file. Shares are fractions (0.25 is 25 %), and values marked (provisional) are placeholders that may still change in balancing. Inventions are shown by id and name; see Research data.

Naval (domain/naval/spec.md, naval design v1). The design names one naval config file; keys follow spec section 10. Every number is a design placeholder. Not given by the design (left out): bounty per sailor, muster time, fish-lost formula (OQ-NAVY-14), wind calendars, storm and freeze seasons, pirate season length (OQ-NAVY-23).

#Blockade (provisional)

#Starting fleets (provisional)

F-115: realms that had ships in 1444 start with them, by the catalogue's naval_capability (very_low, low, moderate, high, very_high). Warships are the strongest the realm could order in its best port; transports are its sea's trade ship. Ages are drawn up to maxAgeYears. Ports west of noShipsWestOfLon (the Americas, whose 1444 realms had canoes and rafts, not cogs, junks or dhows) start with none. Provisional.

#Hired fleets (provisional)

F-115: fleets for hire, as free companies are for the land (WAR-213). forHire free fleets at once, each moving on at moveOnChance a month; one comes when it can reach a port of the hirer within reachDays. Pay a month is the ships' upkeep and crews' wages at sea times payMultiplier (free) or lentPayMultiplier (a realm's own fleet, the gold going to that realm). Realms whose naval_capability is in lenderLevels lend an idle fleet in port with at least lenderMinWarships warships. The AI hires when at war and outgunned, keeping reserveMonths of pay. Captains bring skills of 1 to captainSkillMax. Provisional.

#Game (provisional)

Fits naval's numbers to this game's treasury, as defines' goldScale does for recruiting and works: ship gold and upkeep times goldScale, sailors' wages and bounty times wageScale, so a sailor costs about what a soldier does (D-NAVY-30). Sea battles (D-NAVY-31): each 4-hour round (X-1) a side deals its ships' strength times roundHits, the first ship of the other side's line taking them; a side whose strength falls to breakShare of its start runs for port. Docking takes dockDays; landing an army a day per menLandedPerDay men (warfare 11.3). All provisional.

#Upgrades (provisional)

The upgrade path (D-NAVY-33): a port no longer offers a ship type once it can build the type that replaces it, or any type further along. Trade: the sea's cog, junk or dhow, then carrack, fluyt, steamer. War: galley or war canoe fleet, then armed carrack, galleon, ship of the line; the turtle ship gives way to the ship of the line. Exploration ships have no successor yet. Provisional.

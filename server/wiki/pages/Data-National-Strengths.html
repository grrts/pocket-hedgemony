National strengths data

Every value in the game's national strengths config, exactly as the game reads it. This page is made from packages/rules/data/domain/national-strengths.json each time the config changes, so it always matches the game. How these values are used is explained on National Strengths.

Keys are written as in the config file. Shares are fractions (0.25 is 25 %), and values marked (provisional) are placeholders that may still change in balancing. Inventions are shown by id and name; see Research data.

#Heydays

A heyday wakes when its age is active and its 'wakes' condition holds. It sleeps while 'wakes' fails, and ends for good when its age ends or 'ends' holds. A heyday that ran 25 years in total leaves a mark: a permanent echo worth a third of its points (the 'mark' block, or its first lever at a third of its value).

comment\_mark: D-HER-14: a mark is a legacy and counts toward max_legacies.

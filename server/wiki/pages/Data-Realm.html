Keys are written as in the config file. Shares are fractions (0.25 is 25 %), and values marked (provisional) are placeholders that may still change in balancing. Inventions are shown by id and name; see Research data.

#Assassination (provisional)

Sending an assassin after one of your lords (REALM-280 to REALM-285). gold: what it costs, paid whether or not it works. chance: the base chance it works; less by great_lord_penalty for each Great lord reason (REALM-162), never below min_chance. He dies through the shared death rules (people, REALM-273): his heir or a kinsman takes his lands at heir_loyalty, or with no one left they go back to the crown. #People (provisional)

REALM-270 to REALM-279: one ageing and death model for every person the game names. Each January everyone ages a year and dies with the chance of his age band (age under the first number, chance a year), times his kind's death_mult. Battle and assassination deaths go through the same path. start_age draws a person's age when the game first meets him (a seeded lord or commander, a newly hired official); a lord aged birth_ages without an heir gains one at birth_per_year; a lord starts with an heir at heir_at_start; a lord who dies heirless is followed by a kinsman (kinsman_age) at kinsman_share, else his line dies out. With ai_refills_posts an AI realm, or a lord, fills a dead official's post at once with a new man of his type.

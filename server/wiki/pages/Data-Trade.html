Trade data

Every value in the game's trade config, exactly as the game reads it. This page is made from packages/rules/data/domain/trade.json each time the config changes, so it always matches the game. How these values are used is explained on Trade.

Keys are written as in the config file. Shares are fractions (0.25 is 25 %), and values marked (provisional) are placeholders that may still change in balancing. Inventions are shown by id and name; see Research data.

#Game (provisional)

D-TRADE-20, the game's first slice of trade (provisional). value_scale turns goods gold into the treasury's gold so the world's trade income in 1444 is about what the old flat trade line paid; purse_scale does the same for purses. Craftsmen make crafts (finished goods) and the food surplus is grain until the craft and food buildings make their own wares. The AI looks for loops within its reach in days, counts a quiet road as this ratio, and founds at most one trader a year while it has fewer than this many times its starting traders, plus one. A loop bringing at least ai_keep_route_over_upkeep times its upkeep is kept at the yearly look.

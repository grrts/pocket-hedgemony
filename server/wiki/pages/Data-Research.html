Research data

Every value in the game's research config, exactly as the game reads it. This page is made from packages/rules/data/domain/research.json each time the config changes, so it always matches the game. How these values are used is explained on Research.

Keys are written as in the config file. Shares are fractions (0.25 is 25 %), and values marked (provisional) are placeholders that may still change in balancing. Inventions are shown by id and name; see Research data.

Research (domain/research/spec.md). Era costs, rates and seats are placeholders from the research tree and country stats v3 section 7. Inventions are the 174 rows of the tree (research-tree.md), one config row each. The 13 past inventions of eras A and M come first (RSCH-092); start groups decide each map nation's knowledge at the start (RSCH-098). Era I runs from the start (1378 at the earliest) to 1500; `starts` holds what differs for a start other than 11 November 1444 (RSCH-114), and `era_pace` paces Era I to the start (RSCH-113).

# The 1378 start: Arabia and Barqa (stand-in)

No research thread covered the Arabian part of the catalogue region `as_levant_egypt_arabia`, so the Hejaz, Yemen, Oman and Barqa (eastern Libya) had no 1378 owners when the regions were joined. (`east` holds the Mamluks with Egypt and the Levant.) This folder is a **stand-in** made at the join (milestone 10, step 5) so the scenario has no hole there:

- **Map nations and owner rules** are the 1444 ones for the Hejaz, Yemen and Oman, unchanged, and Barqa (LBY east of 19°E) goes to `mamluks` as in 1444 (low: Mamluk rule there was loose). Libya west of 19°E is left to `tripoli_regency` (africa-americas).
- **Rulers on 20 September 1378:** the Sharif of Mecca is Ahmad ibn Ajlan, sole emir from 1375 (medium). Yemen is the Zaydi highland imamate of al-Nasir Muhammad Salah al-Din (1372–91), as the 1444 map's Zaydi Yemen (medium); the Rasulid lowlands are not split off. Oman's Nabhani ruler is unknown (null).
- **Culture** is carried from 1444.
- No relations or subjects are added here. The Hejaz as a Mamluk vassal (`checkpoints/1378.json`) is left for the research pass.

A proper research pass for this region should replace these files.
